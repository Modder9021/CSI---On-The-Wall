# On the Wall

On the Wall is a top-down psychological mystery RPG where players explore a choice-driven story about accountability, guilt, and consequences.

## Audience

On the Wall is designed for Gen Z players ages 13-30 who enjoy short narrative mysteries, RPG exploration, and choice-based stories.

## Planned gameplay

- Explore a top-down map and interact with NPCs and objects.
- Collect clues and review them in an inventory.
- Make dialogue choices that affect flashbacks and endings.
- Solve clue-based puzzles to unlock new story areas.
